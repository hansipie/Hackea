## This is Next.js 16

This version has breaking changes: APIs, conventions and file structure may differ from your training data. Read the relevant guide in `node_modules/next/dist/docs/` before writing any code, and heed deprecation notices. (`agentRules: false` in `next.config.ts` keeps `next dev` from rewriting this file.)

## House rules

- No em dashes or en dashes anywhere: copy, code, comments, commit messages. Use a comma, colon or parentheses.
- One voice only: OpenAI `marin` (`gpt-4o-mini-tts`), for recorded clips and live lines alike (`src/lib/voice/tts.ts`). Never fall back to the device's speech.
- IKEA's raw listings (`scripts/ikea/facts*.json`, `scripts/ikea/cache/`) stay out of git; only the rows built from them in `src/data` ship.
- Every guide is generated by `src/lib/plan`; never copy IKEA's manuals or images.
